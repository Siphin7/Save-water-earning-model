Earth's Water Reality

Only 3% of
Earth's water is
freshwater

Of all the water on our blue planet, 97% is saltwater in our oceans. Of the remaining 3%, most is locked in glaciers and ice caps. A mere 0.3% is accessible in rivers, lakes, and groundwater.

Yet we treat this finite, irreplaceable resource as if it were infinite. Industrial agriculture uses 70% of all freshwater, while millions walk miles daily for a single glass.

How you can make a difference

Shorter Showers

Reduce shower time from 10 to 5 minutes. Every minute you cut saves roughly 10 litres of water — a simple act with a massive cumulative impact.

Fix Every Leak

A dripping faucet wastes 15,000+ litres per year. A leaky toilet can waste 200,000 litres. Regular inspection and swift repair prevents staggering losses.
